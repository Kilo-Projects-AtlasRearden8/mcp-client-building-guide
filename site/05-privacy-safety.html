5. Privacy and Safety Rules

Building MCP Clients · Asdale Dynamics, LLC

Privacy first

All examples in this manual are local-first: MCP servers run as processes on your computer, files are read from your disk, and nothing is uploaded to a third party just to use an MCP server. No API keys, secrets, or personal data are needed for any example.

Golden rules

  • Share specific folders, never your whole computer.
  • Never share folders with secrets (passwords, keys, IDs, tax, medical, bank data).
  • Prefer read-only servers.
  • You are in charge: stop or remove a server at any time.
  • Assume the AI provider sees what you send it.

Path-traversal protection

The example filesystem server confines all access to a single root folder and rejects attempts to escape it (../etc/passwd).

Healthcare note

This guide is for education only. It is never medical advice. If you are a healthcare worker, do not put real patient data into an AI or an MCP server unless you have confirmed it is permitted by your organization and by applicable privacy law.
